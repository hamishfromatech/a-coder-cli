Questions

Straight answers

Is the software really free forever?

For individuals, solo founders, and businesses with less than USD $10M in total annual revenue — yes, forever, MIT open source, no trials, no expiring features. Organizations above that threshold use A-Coder under The A-Tech Corporation License for company-wide use, which funds the free tier rather than gating features.

What counts as an enterprise?

A business with USD $10,000,000 or more in total annual revenue, including parent companies, subsidiaries, and affiliates. If you're below that — solo founder, startup, small or medium business — you're on the free tier permanently. No revenue verification at install time either; the license simply asks you to count honestly.

So what do I actually pay for?

Only models and external services: your own provider API keys (paid to those providers, not to us), the Amaara Cloud Coding plan if you want Australian-hosted frontier models, A-Coder Pro if you want the curated toolkit plus Senix reviews, and Tandem if a project needs human engineers. Enterprises pay for The A-Tech Corporation License itself. Everything else is free.

Can I run A-Coder with zero spend?

Yes. Point A-Coder at local models — Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM, or llama.cpp are auto-discovered — and run with --offline to disable all network extras. Every feature of the software works. Total cost: whatever your own hardware costs to run, nothing more.

Why give the software away?

Sovereign AI begins with sovereign software. A-Coder is part of Project Infra's movement: the tools themselves should never be the tollbooth for builders. We keep the software free for individuals and small-to-medium business, and charge enterprises at scale and hosted services at honest prices — that's the whole model.
