03 / CLI

The agent that lives in your terminal.

Self-extensible by design. It discovers your MCP servers, skills, and extensions at launch — and you can teach it new behavior mid-session. Background agents keep long jobs running while you keep working.

  • ▸Uniform tool results — glyph, timing, stats for every call, expandable inline.
  • ▸Parallel sub-agents for search-heavy work, live status rail for background jobs.
  • ▸Fresh model catalog every time you open the picker.
  • ▸Runs anywhere Node 22+ or Bun does. One binary, no daemon.
